How SMARTLIB Works

Borrowing academic and research resources has never been faster. Four simple steps from discovery to reading.

1

1. Browse & Search

Explore thousands of titles with real-time availability filters, ISBN queries, and recommendations.

2

2. Reserve Online

Lock your copy online with one click. Get instant email notifications as soon as books are ready.

3

3. Collect with 3D Map

Walk straight to the exact floor, section, rack, and shelf using our interactive 3D spatial guide.

4

4. Enjoy & Return

Check out instantly with barcode scanning, track due dates on your dashboard, and renew seamlessly.
